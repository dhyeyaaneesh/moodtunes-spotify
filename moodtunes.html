Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What MoodTunes is

MoodTunes is a mood-based music discovery app. It helps you find songs that match how you feel, and lets you analyse any song’s audio features to determine its mood. It is a personal project, not a commercial service.

2. Data we collect

Account data — If you create an account, your username, email address, password (not transmitted anywhere — stored only in your own browser’s localStorage), and optional bio and profile picture are saved locally on your device only. We have no server. None of this data is sent to us.

Spotify data — When you connect Spotify, you authorise us to access your Spotify account using OAuth 2.0 PKCE. We only request the user-read-private scope, which allows us to call the Spotify search and audio-features APIs on your behalf. We do not store your Spotify username, email, or any listening history. Your Spotify access token is stored only in your browser’s localStorage and expires after 1 hour.

No analytics, no tracking — We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or any third-party analytics service.

3. Third-party services

Spotify — Song search and audio features are provided by the Spotify Web API. When you use the analyser, your queries are sent to Spotify’s servers. Spotify’s own Privacy Policy applies to that data.

Google Fonts — This app loads the Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address per Google’s privacy policy.

YouTube — Clicking the play button on a song recommendation opens a YouTube search in a new tab. YouTube’s privacy policy applies there.

4. Data storage

All app data (account, Spotify token, preferences) is stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data or clicking “Log out” / “Disconnect” within the app.

5. Children’s privacy

MoodTunes is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Your rights

Since all data is stored locally in your browser, you have full control over it. To delete everything: clear your browser’s localStorage for this site. To disconnect Spotify: click “Disconnect” in the analyser tab — this removes your access token immediately.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the “Last updated” date above will be updated. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions, please open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository or contact the developer directly.
